2.4 Eligibility check

9:41

Eligibility check

Question 3 of 6 · about a minute left

Have you had a tattoo or piercing in the last 12 months?

New tattoos and piercings carry a small risk of infection that tests may not pick up yet. If yes, you can donate 12 months after getting it.

Why do we ask this?

Blood centres in India follow National Blood Transfusion Council rules. They protect both you and the patient who receives your blood.
